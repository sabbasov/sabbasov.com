5 Composition Rules — Repeat on Every Shot

1. IntersectionSubject at rule-of-thirds intersection. Never dead center unless symmetry is intentional.
2. Leading lineFind a natural line (trail, river, ridge, road) from foreground to subject.
3. Foreground anchorInclude something within 3 feet of camera. Rocks, gear, paddle. Adds depth.
4. Sky ratioDramatic sky: 2/3 sky, 1/3 land. Boring sky: 1/3 sky, 2/3 land. Never 50/50.
5. Change heightGet low or get high first. Eye-level is the forgettable default.
Audio field rules: Wind above ~10 mph → don't attempt voice. Biking and fast kayaking → music-only edit. Always record 30–60 seconds of ambient silence at each major location. Record 2+ minutes of fire audio with no talking.

Post-Field

Default: do this the same night. These 3 steps take 5–10 minutes and prevent all end-of-month backlog. The BRIEF note is the most skipped and most important.

Standard (every trip — 5–10 min)

1
Transfer footage + photos to external drive
SD card → 01_RAW/VIDEO_GoPro/. iPhone → 01_RAW/VIDEO_iPhone/. DJI → 01_RAW/AUDIO_DJI/. iPhone photos → 02_PHOTOS_RAW/iPhone_ProRAW/. Plug in, copy, done. Do not leave footage on SD card.
2
Queue GoPro cloud backup + format card
Open GoPro app on WiFi → confirm auto-upload running. Then format SD card in-camera (not delete — format). This is your offsite backup confirmation step.
3
Add one line to BRIEF.md
Open YYYY-MM_BRIEF.md. Add: YYYY-MM-DD · [Location] · [activities] · [one notable thing]. That's it. Two minutes. Do not skip this — it's what makes end-of-month writing fast.

If you have 5 more minutes (optional, not required)

+
Quick photo reject pass
Open iPhone_ProRAW folder. Delete only obvious failures: blurry, accidental, overexposed beyond recovery. Don't select — just reject clear garbage. Saves 15 minutes at month-end.
Default decision: If you're exhausted, do steps 1 and 2 only. Step 3 (BRIEF note) can happen the next morning. Never skip the transfer.

Editing System

All editing in DaVinci Resolve. Three timelines per month: SELECTS_Master → FILM_Monthly → SHORT_###.

Session 1 — Import and Flag (target: 2 hours)

1
Create new DaVinci project: YYYY-MM_MonthName
Import all raw footage. Create bins: RAW_GoPro / RAW_iPhone / RAW_Audio. Never edit from raw bins.
2
Review pass — color flag all clips
Scrub at 2–3× speed in Media Pool. Green = use. Yellow = maybe. Red = reject. Target: reduce footage by 70%. Do not cut or trim yet — just flag.
3
Build SELECTS_Master timeline
Drag all green clips in rough chronological order. Don't cut yet. Target: 8–15 minutes of selects for a 1–3 minute film.

Session 2 — Monthly Film Cut (target: 2–3 hours)

Create timeline: FILM_Monthly_v1. Build the story to this structure:

SegmentDurationContent
Opening hook0:00–0:20Best single shot of the month. No context needed — pure visual impact. Cut to beat 1 of music.
Establish location0:20–0:40Wide shots: where you are, road trip arrival, trail start, put-in point.
Activity builds0:40–1:40Core footage. Mix real-time + slow-mo. Include one ambient audio scene.
Emotional peak1:40–2:10Summit, best water shot, firelight. The moment of the month.
Resolution2:10–endPack-up, drive out, sunset, something quiet.

Cutting Rules

Cut on motionAlways cut mid-movement — mid-step, mid-paddle, mid-swing. Motion carries the cut invisibly.
Clip durationsWide establishing: 3–5 sec. Action: 1–2 sec max. Close-up: 2–3 sec. Violating these is the most common amateur error.
Slow-mo limitMaximum 2–3 slow-mo clips per monthly film. Place at emotional peak only. Overused = no impact.
Music firstCut to music, then adjust clips. If you cut visually first, you'll spend 3× longer on pacing.
One storyIf you filmed 4 activities, pick the strongest arc. The others become supporting scenes, not co-equal storylines.

Color Grading — DaVinci Color Page

Apply the same 4-node structure to every clip. Save a still after grading the first clip and apply to all others.

Node AD-Log M LUT
→
Node BExposure/Contrast
→
Node CColor Balance
→
Node DSaturation/HSL
NodeWhat to Adjust
A — LUTApply GoPro D-Log M → REC.709 LUT. This is your neutral starting point.
B — PrimaryLift shadows +5–8. Lower highlights −5–10. Use Parade scope — no clipping.
C — ColorWarm midtones slightly (+amber in Log Wheels). Cool highlights slightly (blue-teal). Subtle — less than you think.
D — SaturationReduce overall −10–15%. Then selectively boost: Greens +5 (forest), Blues +5 (water). Protect skin tones on vectorscope.
Grading errors to avoid: Over-saturated GoPro greens (reduce them, don't boost). Crushed blacks (lift slightly). Teal-and-orange overuse. Mismatched grades between activity types in the same film. Grading D-Log M without applying the LUT first.

Website Publishing — Simplified (3 steps, ~5 min)

Copy last month's archive page, rename it
In your GitHub repo: duplicate archive/YYYY-MM.html → rename to new month. All formatting, layout, and structure is already there.
2
Replace 5 things only
Title text. YouTube embed URL. Photo filenames (drag new photos into repo /photos/ folder). 2-sentence summary (use Marketing Director Claude output). Month label in nav. Nothing else changes.

Audio Mix — DaVinci Fairlight Page

Music busBase level −18 dB. Duck to −28 dB under voice or impactful audio.
Ambient bus−24 dB. Mix underneath music for depth.
Voice bus0 dB. Apply High Pass Filter at 80 Hz to all outdoor voice clips (removes wind rumble).
Peak checkFinal mix should peak at −1 dBFS maximum. Export should not clip.

Photo System

5-Step Monthly Selection (target: 30 minutes)

1
Import all to Lightroom Classic
Create named album: YYYY-MM_MonthName. Import ProRAW from iPhone and RAW from GoPro.
2
First pass — flag and reject only (10 min)
Go fast. P = keep, X = reject. Don't agonize. Target: reduce total by 60%.
3
Second pass — star rating
3★ = definitely in. 2★ = maybe. 1★ = archive only (never featured).
4
Final 10: one per activity + standouts
One hiking, one water, one biking, one camping, one road trip, plus 2–3 pure landscape/mood shots that are genuinely excellent. Variety of activities, variety of aspect ratios.
5
Grade selects only
Apply OUTDOOR_Base preset to all 10. Micro-adjust per photo (2–3 min each). Everything else stays as raw in archive.

The Marketing Director does two things: plans how your footage gets packaged and framed, then produces all copy for every platform. You send the BRIEF; they return everything ready to paste.

Only what the Marketing Director actually needs. Delete any line that doesn't apply this month. The ARC line is the most important — write it first.
MONTH: [Month YYYY] ACTIVITIES: [Hiking · Kayaking · Biking · Camping · Road trip] — [Location(s)] ARC: [One sentence: what is this month actually about?] FILM: [filename] · [duration] · [YouTube link after upload] SHORTS: 001 · [activity] · [duration]s · [one-line description] 002 · [activity] · [duration]s · [one-line description] 003 · [activity] · [duration]s · [one-line description] PHOTOS (in Photos_Exported/): 001 · [description] · [website header / IG post / Stories / gallery] 002 · [description] · [placement] [one line per photo, 10 total — or 5 in low energy month] NOTES: [tone, angle, timing, anything to avoid] MODE: [standard / minimal month]

Passing to Marketing Director — One Step

Paste the completed brief into the Marketing Director chat. They return in one response: content angle recommendation, YouTube title + description + tags, one Reels caption per short (3 tones), website headline + summary, and alt text for all photos. Copy directly to platforms.

Recovery Mode

Use this when: you missed 2+ weeks of post-field hygiene, you have too much footage, or the month-end is here and you feel overwhelmed.

Situation: Too Much Footage

1
Do not open DaVinci yet
First decision: pick one trip only. Not the one with the most footage — the one with the best single shot or the most interesting story. Everything else becomes optional B-roll.
2
Import only that trip's footage into DaVinci
Leave the rest in the raw bins. You're not using it this month. It goes into archive. You may use it next month as B-roll or opening shot.
3
Flag pass: 10 clips max from that trip
Ruthless. 10 clips. First-gut-reaction green flags only. If you have to think about it, it's yellow — skip yellow in recovery mode.
4
Build the film from those 10 clips
Use the 3-part Low Energy structure: opener → sequences → closer. Target 60–90 seconds. Don't aim for your best film — aim for a complete, published film.

Situation: Skipped 2 Weeks of Post-Field Steps

What got skippedRecovery actionTime
Footage not transferredTransfer everything now in one batch. Sort into correct subfolders.20 min
No BRIEF.md notesWrite trip notes from memory now — even 1 sentence per trip. Approximate dates are fine.5 min
Photos not reviewedSkip the reject pass entirely. Go straight to month-end selection from full photo pool.0 min (skip)
GoPro cloud not backed upPlug in + connect to WiFi now. Let it run.2 min

Situation: Feeling Lazy (Most Common)

The rule: open DaVinci and spend 20 minutes doing nothing but flagging. Don't edit, don't grade, don't open the color page. Just flag. Momentum builds from the smallest action. If after 20 minutes you still want to stop, stop — and come back tomorrow. The flagging is saved.

The "Good Enough" Standard

A published film with 3 average clips beats an unpublished film with 10 great clips. The archive is built on consistency. When in doubt, use Low Energy Mode and publish what you have.

Month published, anything✓ System intact. Streak continues.
Month partially published (film only, no shorts)✓ Still counts. Shorts can come later.
Month skipped entirely✗ Archive gap. Harder to restart. Don't let this happen twice in a row.
